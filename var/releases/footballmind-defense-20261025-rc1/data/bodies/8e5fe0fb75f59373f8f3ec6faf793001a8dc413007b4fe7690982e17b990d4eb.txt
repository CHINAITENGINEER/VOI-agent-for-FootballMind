Enzo Maresca has named a strong side to face Morecambe in the third round of the FA Cup as Reece James and Romeo Lavia are included in the starting XI.

The Blues boss has made ten changes to the side that drew 1-1 with Crystal Palace a week ago, with Pedro Neto the only one to retain his place.

Filip Jorgensen continues this season's trend of starting in goal for cup competitions and he will be protected by a back four of James, Axel Disasi , Tosin Adarabioyo and Renato Veiga.

Lavia will line up in midfield, as Joao Felix and Christopher Nkunku also feature, with Pedro Neto and Tyrique George set to play on the flanks.

Marc Guiu should lead the line as the Blues take on a Morecambe side that are second from bottom in League Two.

Chelsea: Jorgensen; James (c), Disasi, Tosin, Veiga; Lavia, Joao Felix; Neto, Nkunku, George; Guiu

Substitutes: Sanchez, Gusto, Acheampong, Colwill, Cucurella, Fernandez, Palmer, Sancho, Jackson
