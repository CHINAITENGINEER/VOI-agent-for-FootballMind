Parma, 22 January 2025 - Milan Djurić is Gialloblu. Parma Calcio can announce that the forward has joined from AC Monza on a permanent deal. Originally from Bosnia but an Italian citizen, Milan is a centre-forward who knows how to exploit his great technical and physical skills, especially in the air, a characteristic that allows him to support his sides in attack. In recent Serie A seasons, he's put in big campaigns with Salernitana, Hellas Verona and AC Monza. His strength and determination will be fundamental to his journey in Gialloblu. Milan Djurić's statement: “ I am very happy to join a club with an extraordinary history like Parma. I will try to give my all like I have always done in my career, I will bring my skills and characteristics to serve the Coach and the side. I will help out with my experience so that all the lads with great potential can continue to grow. I am very proud of this choice and I am already focused on our team objectives '. Sporting Director, Mauro Pederzoli, spoke: “ We are pleased to welcome Milan, he brings experience and desire to our side. We chose him as he has the physical and technical characteristics to be able to boost the side in attack, we know it will certainly be a great source of motivation for him to be in an environment such as Parma'. #BenvenutoMilan
