On Saturday, as our WSL champions celebrated their victory over Liverpool Women and lifted the trophy, the Events team at Chelsea Football Club hosted Beyond the Game at Stamford Bridge.

This insightful event brought together leaders from the events industry and trailblazers from women’s football to explore the powerful parallels between these two fast-evolving fields, all while enjoying elite football and brunch hospitality.

From redefining fan experiences and boosting attendances, to championing representation and overcoming structural barriers, the conversations highlighted how the events industry and women’s football can gain insights from each other.

Chelsea’s Events team would like to say a huge thank you to all our speakers and guests, who made the day a success.
