Parma, 19 February 2025 - Bologna are Parma's opposition on Matchday 26 of Serie A Enilive 2024/25, scheduled for Saturday 22 February at Parma's Tardini Stadium (15:00 CET, live on DAZN). Here are the statistics ahead of the match: Parma and Bologna have drawn 16 of their 37 Serie A meetings (9 gialloblu wins and 12 wins for the rossoblù). Parma have only drawn more matches in the top flight aganist Fiorentina (19) and Juventus (17). Bologna are unbeaten in their last ten games against Parma in Serie A (4W, 6D) - the gialloblu's last win in the league against Bologna dates back to 22 December 2012. Parma completed a 2-1 comeback at the Dall'Ara thanks to Jaime Valdés and Nicola Sansone's goals following Frederik Sörensen's opener. Bologna won their last away game against Parma in the league (3-0, 7 February 2021) and they have never won back to back at the Tardini in Serie A history. Bologna is one of the three Serie A sides that are unbeaten since the turn of the new year (3W, 4D), alongside Roma and Napoli. The rossoblu could avoid defeat in their first 8 matches of the year for the first time since 2005 (4W, 4D in this case). While Bologna have faced the least amount of shots (227) in this Serie A season, only Empoli (381) have faced more than Parma (377). Parma and Bologna are two of the four teams to have lost the most amount of points to goals scored in the last 20 minutes of games in the league this season (9, alongside Como and Venezia).
