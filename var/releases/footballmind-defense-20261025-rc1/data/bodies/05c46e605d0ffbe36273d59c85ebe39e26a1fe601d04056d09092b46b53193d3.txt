Parma, 27 March 2025 - Parma Calcio announces that ticket sales for Parma vs. Inter, on Matchday 31 of Serie A Enilive (Saturday 5 April at 18:00 CEST), will be live from Monday 31 March at 10:00 CET. The sales will be divided into four phases for our match against Inter. PHASE 1 | 2024/25 SEASON-TICKET HOLDERS From 10:00 CET until 23:59 CET on Monday 31 March, tickets will only be available to 2024/25 season-ticket holders: all members will be able to purchase up to two tickets for themselves or other Gialloblu fans. PHASE 2 | 2024/25 SEASON-TICKET HOLDERS AND TARDINI CARD HOLDERS From 10:00 CEST until 23:59 CEST on Tuesday 1 April, Tardini Card holders (subscribed before 15 March 2025), will be able to buy one ticket for themselves or for another Gialloblu fan. PHASE 3 | CRÉDIT AGRICOLE From 8:30 CEST until 23:59 CEST on Wednesday 2 April, sales are only available to Crédit Agricole account holders at branches from Parma and its surrounding areas. These tickets can be purchased online using a promo code that you can get by contacting your local branch. PHASE 4 | GENERAL SALE From Thursday 3 April (from 10:00 CEST), up until matchday (Saturday 5 April), ticket sales will open up to the public in the sectors remaining.

Parma, 27 March 2025 - Parma Calcio announces that ticket sales for Parma vs. Inter, on Matchday 31 of Serie A Enilive (Saturday 5 April at 18:00 CEST), will be live from Monday 31 March at 10:00 CET.

The sales will be divided into four phases for our match against Inter.

PHASE 1 | 2024/25 SEASON-TICKET HOLDERS

From 10:00 CET until 23:59 CET on Monday 31 March, tickets will only be available to 2024/25 season-ticket holders: all members will be able to purchase up to two tickets for themselves or other Gialloblu fans.

PHASE 2 | 2024/25 SEASON-TICKET HOLDERS AND TARDINI CARD HOLDERS

From 10:00 CEST until 23:59 CEST on Tuesday 1 April, Tardini Card holders (subscribed before 15 March 2025), will be able to buy one ticket for themselves or for another Gialloblu fan.

From 8:30 CEST until 23:59 CEST on Wednesday 2 April, sales are only available to Crédit Agricole account holders at branches from Parma and its surrounding areas. These tickets can be purchased online using a promo code that you can get by contacting your local branch.

From Thursday 3 April (from 10:00 CEST), up until matchday (Saturday 5 April), ticket sales will open up to the public in the sectors remaining.
