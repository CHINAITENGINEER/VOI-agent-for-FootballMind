Collecchio, 16 May 2025 - Morning training session for the Gialloblù who continued their preparations ahead of the Napoli clash, scheduled for Sunday at 20:45 CEST. The team, on the orders of Coach Cristian Chivu and the technical staff, took part in physical and technical activation exercises, general tactical drills, a serie of mini-matches and a match on a reduced-size pitch. The team will train again on the morning on Saturday 17 May.
