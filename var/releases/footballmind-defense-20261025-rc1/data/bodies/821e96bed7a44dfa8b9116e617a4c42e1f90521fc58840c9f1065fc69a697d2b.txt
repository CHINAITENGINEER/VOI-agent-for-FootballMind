Parma, 3 May 2025 - Enrico Delprato's words , after Parma vs. Como, on Serie A Matchday 35. “ It's a loss that hurts as we created chances and stayed in the game. We lacked a goal. Games like the one against Como are ones that when you get ahead, you take something from the match. Unfortunately, we must accept these games too, and we must stay together and concentrated to move forward. The contact in the area in the 91st minute? I I felt the Como defender's studs and I went down, I don't know exactly what happened with the ball, if he won it first, but the referee told me to get up, but I did get studded. However, with the videos there are now, they could have taken a minute to stop play and to review the decision, I thought that would have been fair. It went this way, they will have seen it another way. But I don't know, it's never that clear. I don't think there is a clear line for these sorts of decisions. In the first time we suffered a bit with their dribbling and we struggled a little bit on the ball. Despite everything, we had two clear chances. In the second half we did better in terms of chances created and chances to get ahead. Then we went behind and we didn't manage to turn it around, despite 3-4 chances on goal. We are disappointed as we didn't deserve to lose the game, but this is football. At times you have a normal game which should and as a draw, and you win, against Como it was the opposite. But we must be strong, we have had a good unbeaten run, and we must keep our heads as it is still in our hands. Man? Dennis works well throughout the week, and I am sad for him as we know he has had a difficult spell, however we want the best for him as he is our teammate and he always works well. He should continue this way and he will certainly get back to his best. Now we must reset to go to Empoli and play well with the same concentration that we have had until today. We need to regather energy as it was a tough game. It will be a key game so we must prepare as best as possible'.
