RC Celta will host UD Las Palmas tomorrow , Monday 31 March, at 21:00h . For this matchday number 29 of LALIGA EA SPORTS, which will be played at Abanca Balaídos , Claudio Giráldez has called up 23 of his players.

Williot Swedberg ( 19 ) will be absent through injury for this match.
