Serie A has announced a time change for the 29th matchday game between Roma and Cagliari, scheduled for Sunday, March 16 at the “Olimpico” stadium. In accordance with the decisions of the Provincial Committee for Public Order and Safety, held today in Rome, the match will now start at 4:00 PM instead of 3:00 PM.

Preparations continue ahead of Saturday’s match against Bologna at the Unipol Domus; Yael Trepy trains with the group.
