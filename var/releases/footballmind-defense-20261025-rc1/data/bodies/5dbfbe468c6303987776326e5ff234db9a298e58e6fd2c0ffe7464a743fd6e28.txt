There's nothing like feeling the rivalry and importance of a north London derby in person, and next month you can do just that when Arsenal Women take on Tottenham Hotspur in the Women's Super League.

Over 55,000 tickets have already been sold for clash at Emirates Stadium, which takes place on Sunday, February 16 at 12:30pm. Last season's derby was a sold-out affair so make sure you're part of the action in N5 this time around for what should be another memorable day in N5 as we aim to maintain our proud record against our neighbours.

After beating them 3-0 at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in November, we'll be looking to repeat the feat in the home leg to complete the double over our local rivals, and maintain our push under new head coach Renee Slegers.

Tickets start at £16 for adults and £8 for juniors. Supporters can purchase up to 12 tickets per transaction and a 15% discount is available for general admission and club-level group bookings of six tickets or more. To take up this offer, use GROUP6 in the promotional code box at the checkout.
