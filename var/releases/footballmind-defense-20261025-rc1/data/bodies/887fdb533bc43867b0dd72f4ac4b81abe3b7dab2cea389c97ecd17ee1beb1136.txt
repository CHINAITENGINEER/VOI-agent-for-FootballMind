Originally due to take place on Tuesday 25 February (7.45pm), the fixture is now scheduled for Wednesday 26 February (7.30pm).

Despite Christian Nørgaard's first-half red card, Brentford secured their first Premier League clean sheet and first away point of the season, as they drew 0-0 with Everton in the reverse fixture at Goodison Park in November.

Mark Flekken made some crucial saves to deny the Toffees, while his team-mates Ethan Pinnock and Nathan Collins also contributed to a solid defensive display.
