Parma, 18 May 2025 - Hernani Jr's words after Parma 0-0 Napoli on Serie A Enilive 2024/25 Matchday 37. “ It was a tense and difficult game, but we played an intense game in all departments, we gave everything, we wanted to win, but Napoli are the strongest team in the league. We played a good game, and we approached it in the right way. We must hold onto this important point. The other results? I saw them after our game. We didn't know what was going to happen with the other matches so we needed to stay focused on our goals. Is it all down to the last day? Yes.. It has been that sort of year, but we are alive and we are showing our will to grow and to say in this league and we will fight until the end because we have the right qualities to stay in Serie A. Now we have our last match (against Atalanta in Bergamo), we must keep working and believing in what we are doing. We will need to have the right spirit like we had against Napoli, with humility and concentration to win as it is all in our hands'.
