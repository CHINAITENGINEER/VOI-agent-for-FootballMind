RC Celta will receive tomorrow, Sunday, May 18, at 19:00h, Rayo Vallecano. For this last home game of the season, belonging to the 37th LALIGA EA SPORTS matchday to be played at Abanca Balaídos, the Celta coach, Claudio Giráldez, has called up 24 players.

The list of called-up players will be formed by Iván Villar ( 1 ), Vicente Guaita ( 13 ), Marc Vidal ( 26 ), Óscar Mingueza ( 3 ), Hugo Sotelo ( 4 ), Sergio Carreira ( 5 ), Ilaix Moriba ( 6 ), Borja Iglesias ( 7 ), Fran Beltrán ( 8 ), Iago Aspas ( 10 ), Franco Cervi ( 11 ), Alfon ( 12 ), Iker Losada ( 14 ), Pablo Durán ( 18 ) , Williot Swedberg ( 19 ), Marcos Alonso ( 20 ), Mihailo Ristic ( 21 ), Hugo Álvarez ( 23 ), Carlos Domínguez ( 24 ), Damián Rodríguez ( 25 ), Fer López ( 28 ), Yoel Lago ( 29 ), Javi Rodríguez ( 32 ) and Miguel Román ( 34 ).

Carl Starfelt ( 2 ) and Jailson Marques ( 16 ) will be absent for this match due to injury and Jones El-Abdellaoui ( 39 ) will be absent due to concentration with his national team in the African Cup.
