Parma, 25 April 2025 - Parma Calcio announces that ticket sales for Parma vs. Como, on Serie Matchday 35 (Saturday 3 May, 15:00 CEST), is now live. UNIVERSITY PROMO “VIVERÈPARMA” For the match against Como, the “ViverÈParma” promo will be active for students of Parma University. With your valid student ID or another document proving your enrollment, you can get a ticket for 10 euros in Curva Sud in any Vivaticket sales point. Find out further information on the “ViverÈParma” promo on the ticketing section of the website.

CHANGING TICKET HOLDER AND ACCESSING THE GROUND
