RC Celta will visit Rayo Vallecano tomorrow , Friday 10 January, at 21:00h . For this 19th LALIGA EA SPORTS matchday, which will be played at the Vallecas Stadium, Claudio Giráldez called up 24 of his players .

The list of called-up players will be as follows Iván Villar ( 1 ), Vicente Guaita ( 13 ), Coke ( 35 ), Carl Starfelt ( 2 ), Óscar Mingueza ( 3 ), Sergio Carreirra ( 5 ), Ilaix Moriba ( 6 ), Borja Iglesias ( 7 ), Fran Beltrán ( 8 ), Tasos Douvikas ( 9 ), Franco Cervi ( 11 ), Alfon ( 12 ), Luca de la Torre ( 14 ), Joseph Aidoo ( 15 ), Jonathan Bamba ( 17 ) , Pablo Durán ( 18 ) , Williot Swedberg ( 19 ), Marcos Alonso ( 20 ) , Javier Manquillo ( 22 ), Carlos Domínguez ( 24 ), Damián Rodríguez ( 25 ), Yoel Lago ( 29 ), Hugo Álvarez ( 30 ) and Hugo Sotelo ( 33 ).

Iago Aspas ( 10 ) and Mihailo Ristic ( 21 ) will be absent through injury .
