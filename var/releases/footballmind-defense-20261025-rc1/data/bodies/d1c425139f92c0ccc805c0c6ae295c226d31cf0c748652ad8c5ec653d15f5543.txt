Here’s some useful information to help you get ready for matchday.

This match is our dedicated Heart of West London fixture.

We’re highlighting the shocking statistic that every week in the UK, 12 young people die from undiagnosed heart conditions.

We want fans at the Gtech to take two minutes to learn the basics of CPR. It may be the most important two minutes of your life.

Our planned activity for matchday includes:

Live CPR demonstrations taking place in our premium lounges. Video tutorials will also be shown on the big screens.

There will be a fan display in the north stand during the players’ walk-out before kick-off. Fans sat in blocks N123 and N124 should take their seats early to be a part of the display.

The team will wear special match shirts during the game, featuring the heart-shaped CPQR code, which when scanned will open a two-minute CPR tutorial video.

Our players will wear special Heart of West London warm-up shirts highlighting the stats around young cardiac death.

Brentford hall of famer Andy Scott and Dr Steve Cox, CEO of Cardiac Risk in the Young, will be pitchside before kick-off chatting to Stu Wakeford about the campaign.

Auctions go live at kick-off. Bid for your favourite shirt on the MatchWornShirts website .

Please take your seat in plenty of time to join in with a minute's applause prior to kick-off in memory of former Manchester City captain and manager Tony Book, who has passed away aged 90.

Book spent eight years as a player at City and captained the side as they celebrated four major trophies inside three years between 1968-1970.
