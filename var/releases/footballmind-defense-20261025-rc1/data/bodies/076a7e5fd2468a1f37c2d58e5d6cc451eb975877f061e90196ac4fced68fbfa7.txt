Collecchio, 11 March 2025 - Morning training session for the Gialloblù who have begun their preparations for the match against Monza on Saturday 15 March at 15:00 CET. On pitch two of the Mutti Training Centre in Collecchio, the team held a strength training session in the gym, took part in physical activation exercises, tactical drills focused on possession, group work for the defensive and attacking phase as well as a match on a reduced-size pitch. The team will take part in a morning training session on Wednesday 12 March.
