Parma 14 February 2025 - Parma Calcio announces that ticket sales for Parma vs. Bologna , on Matchday 26 of Serie A Enilive (Saturday 22 February at 15:00 CET), will be live from Friday 14 February at 11:00 CET. The sale will be divided in three phases for our match against Bologna Football Club 1909. PHASE 1 | 2024/25 MEMBERS AND TARDINI CARD HOLDERS From 11:00 CET on Friday 14 February, tickets will only be available to 2024/25 members: all members will be able to purchase up to four tickets until 23:59 CET on Sunday 16th February. Members can buy up to four tickets and residents of Bologna are excluded from the sale. PHASE 2 | CRÉDIT AGRICOLE From 8:30 CET on Monday 17 February until 23:59 CET on Tuesday 18 February, sales are only available to Crédit Agricole account holders at branches from Parma and its surrounding areas. These tickets can be purchased online using a promo code that you can get by contacting your local branch. Crédit Agricole tickets cannot be obtained by Bologna residents. PHASE 3 | OPEN SALE From 10:00 CET on Wednesday 19 up until matchday (Saturday 22 February), ticket sales will open up to the public in the below sectors. The Curva Sud section will only be available at Vivaticket ticket stores in and around Parma.

Parma 14 February 2025 - Parma Calcio announces that ticket sales for Parma vs. Bologna , on Matchday 26 of Serie A Enilive (Saturday 22 February at 15:00 CET), will be live from Friday 14 February at 11:00 CET.

The sale will be divided in three phases for our match against Bologna Football Club 1909.

PHASE 1 | 2024/25 MEMBERS AND TARDINI CARD HOLDERS

From 11:00 CET on Friday 14 February, tickets will only be available to 2024/25 members: all members will be able to purchase up to four tickets until 23:59 CET on Sunday 16th February. Members can buy up to four tickets and residents of Bologna are excluded from the sale.
