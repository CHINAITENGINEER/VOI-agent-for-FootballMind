Maurizio Mariani will referee Venezia v Napoli; his assistants will be Preti and Perrotti, and Cosso will be the fourth official. Di Paolo and Dionisi will be on VAR duties. It will be Maurizio Mariani’s 167 th Serie A game and his 15 th so far in the 2024/25 season. Mariani has refereed four Venezia games in Serie A. The first was a draw and the following three matches ended in defeat, this season against Udinese on 1 February. Meanwhile Mariani has taken charge of 22 Napoli games, and the record stands at 14 wins, two draws and six defeats. One of these was this season – a 1-1 draw against Inter on 10 November.
