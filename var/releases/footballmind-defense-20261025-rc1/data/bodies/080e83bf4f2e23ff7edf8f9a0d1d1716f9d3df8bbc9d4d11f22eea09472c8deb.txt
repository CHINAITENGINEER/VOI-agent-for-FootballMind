Alisson Becker was voted Liverpool’s Carlsberg Player of the Match in the 1-0 victory away at Paris Saint-Germain.

The goalkeeper was in outstanding form as he made a string of excellent saves to keep the home side at bay throughout Wednesday night’s Champions League last-16 first leg in France.

Substitutes Harvey Elliott and Darwin Nunez then combined in the 87th minute for the former to tuck home a memorable winning goal.

Supporters duly selected Alisson as the team’s top performer in our Match Centre poll.
