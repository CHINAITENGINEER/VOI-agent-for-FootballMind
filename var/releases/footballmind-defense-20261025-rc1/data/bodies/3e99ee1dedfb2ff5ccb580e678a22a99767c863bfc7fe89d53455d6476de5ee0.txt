Two days to Cagliari-Juventus, Mister Nicola’s rossoblù took to the field at the CRAI Sport Center for a new training session ahead of Sunday night’s match. After a technical warm-up, they focused on tactics, working on playing solutions and a series of set pieces.

Customized training for Kingstone, the forward is recovering from the effects of a knock to his left knee.

Tomorrow, Saturday, February 22, the team will train in front of their fans at the Unipol Domus. To attend the session, you need to go to cagliaricalcio.com or the TicketOne website and download the free pass (up to a maximum of two) at this link (CLICK HERE ). The appointment is at 9:30 AM, with the session starting at 10:30 AM: the Curva Nord will be available.

At the end of the training session, Mister Nicola will meet the media to present the match: the conference is scheduled for 12:15 PM and will be streamed live on the Cagliari Calcio app. Simply access the app using the provided link (HERE) and register.
