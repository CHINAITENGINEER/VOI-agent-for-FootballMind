The newly crowned Premier League champions returned to training today as Liverpool prepare for Sunday's trip to Chelsea.

Fresh from being confirmed as the league's winners for 2024-25 at the weekend, Arne Slot's men are now building up for an away clash with the Blues.

Updates around the AXA Training Centre reflecting the club's 20th title in history greeted the Reds, too, as they got back to work in Kirkby.
