If you are heading to the match, here’s some useful information on getting to the ground and tips to make the most of your trip to Leicester.

Direct trains to Leicester station run every 30 minutes from London St Pancras International and take approximately one hour and 10 minutes.

On arrival at Leicester station, bus services 80 and 20 will take you from Leicester city centre to within five minutes’ walk from King Power Stadium. Alternatively, the ground is around a 25-minute walk from the station.

The last direct train back to London from Leicester station is at 10.52pm. Return tickets to London are liable to sell out. We recommend you use Trainline to book your ticket as soon as possible.

Parking at King Power Stadium can be booked in advance of the match. The club car park is on Filbert Street and costs £19 per car. Parking cannot be booked on the day of the match. Find booking information on Leicester’s website .

There are several independent car parks in the surrounding areas that are up to 10 minutes’ walk to the stadium, such as Freemans Common and Leicester Tigers Rugby Club. These car parks typically cost £5 and are first come, first served.

Ticket collection for this fixture is available at the Brentford FC box office at the Gtech Community Stadium until 7pm on Thursday 20 February.

After this time, including on matchday, tickets must be collected from the matchday ticket collection point at the King Power Stadium, located to the left of the Foxes Fan Store. Collection opens 90 minutes before kick-off.

Any duplicate ticket requests must be sent to Leicester City by the Brentford box office. Please contact Brentford's box office with any inquiries ahead of the matchday.

If you’re collecting your ticket at the King Power, please remember to bring ID.

We would also like to remind fans travelling to the King Power to observe and respect the family friendly seating. Anyone who holds a ticket in the family friendly area is reminded that they should be seated for the duration of the match.

The Barley Mow pub, near to Leicester station, welcomes away supporters and will be a place to meet other Bees fans before kick-off.

Please note that there are no designated away pubs in the vicinity of the King Power Stadium. The previously popular location The Counting House is permanently closed.

Entrance to the away section will be in the corner of the north and east stands at turnstiles 40-49, open 90 minutes before kick-off. The accessible entrance is at turnstiles 42 and 43.

Fans will be searched on entry and bags larger than A4 size will not be permitted. There is no bag storage at the ground.

The stadium has a multi-faith prayer room. Please speak to a steward if you would like to use the room.

If you experience or see any form of harassment or discrimination while at the stadium, please speak to the nearest steward. You can report a hate crime by texting ‘FOXES’, followed by your message, seat details and the nature of your concern to 60066.

The King Power Stadium includes wheelchair access for away fans and accessible toilets, with designated accessibility stewards on hand for any support or to provide RADAR keys. Stewards can also help with ordering food and refreshments. Visually impaired fans can request audio-descriptive headsets and access for guide dogs in advance of the game. For any requests and questions, and to enquire about accessible parking, contact Leicester’s Disability Liaison Officer by email at [email protected] .

Staying the night in Leicester? The city is renowned for its nightlife with many restaurants, bars and pubs. For budget hotels, Novotel, Premier Inn and Travelodge have sites in the city centre.

Make the most of the February half-term and enjoy some family fun before the game. Leicester is home to the National Space Centre with interactive galleries and the UK’s largest planetarium. Discover how archaeologists uncovered a lost king at the King Richard III Visitor Centre . Immerse yourself in art at the Van Gogh Immersive Experience . Theme park Drayton Manor is also a short car journey west of the city.

Have a safe trip and enjoy the match!
